L6 · Composition Plan

Complete one row for every positioned, layered, clipped, rounded, or shape-wrapped element. Duplicate rows as needed.

Element Content purpose Normal-flow baseline Chosen technique and why Containing block / stacking context Fallback or disengagement Required test
Issue badge Identifies the article as a community feature. The badge remains readable with the feature header content in normal flow. Relative positioning on the feature header and absolute positioning on the badge anchor the label to the header without using positioning for the main page layout. The .feature-header is the containing block for the absolutely positioned .issue-badge. The badge can return to normal flow or be repositioned if the available space becomes too narrow. Test at 320px, 768px, 1280px, 200% zoom, and with a long title.
Story guide Provides navigation to the four main sections of the feature story. The navigation remains readable in normal source order before the article content. Sticky positioning keeps the story navigation available while the reader moves through the article without removing it from its normal layout area. The story guide remains within the main Grid layout and is bounded by its containing area. If sticky behavior is unavailable or becomes impractical at a smaller viewport, the navigation remains in normal flow. Test at 320px, 768px, 1280px, 200% zoom, keyboard focus, and page/section boundaries.
Pull quote Emphasizes the central idea of the feature story. The quotation remains readable as a normal blockquote in document flow. A controlled overlap gives the quotation editorial emphasis while keeping the surrounding story readable. The pull quote remains within the story content area and uses the story section as its layout context. If the overlap becomes too narrow or interferes with surrounding content, the quote returns to normal flow. Test at 320px, 768px, 1280px, 200% zoom, with doubled paragraph content, and for pointer/focus safety.
